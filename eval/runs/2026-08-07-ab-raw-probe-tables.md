# A/B raw probe output — 2026-08-07

Per-question rank tables behind `2026-08-07-ab-expansion-and-enrichment.md`,
which reports only aggregate recall. Produced by
`python -m eval.retrieval_probe` — retrieval only, the query embedding is the
sole model call.

Each cell is the 1-based rank of the first chunk matching that gold label at
that stage, or `miss`. Q1 and Q2 are skipped: their gold blocks are still
`TBD` pending engineering sign-off.

## Two caveats these numbers carry

**Gold labels are narrower than the clauses.** Q4 and Q5 name a single page
of a multi-page clause (§6.5.4 spans 120–123; Q5's deciding sentence is on
p. 37, not the p. 36 heading), so absolute recall is understated in every
table. Comparisons between arms are unaffected — all four are scored against
identical labels.

**Arms B and D are not exactly reproducible.** Their sub-queries are
generated by a model, so the expansion changes slightly between runs and
fused ranks move with it. Arms A and C are deterministic as of the
`_expand_compounds` tie-break fix committed alongside this file; before it,
every arm drifted between runs because equal-length compound parts were
selected in randomised set order.
