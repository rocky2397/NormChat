## Q1 — gut

Names `ÖNORM EN 1995-1-2` and `ÖNORM B 1995-1-2`, describes the annex as
*"nationale Erläuterungen und Ergänzungen … gemeinsam mit dieser
anzuwenden"*, and adds `B 1991-1-2` and `B 1995-1-1` from the normative
reference list. Five of seven B-document sources are body pages. Complete
and sourced.

## Q2 — mittel

Correct and sourced throughout: `k_mod,fi`, `γ_M,fi`, and
`R_d,t,fi = R₂₀/γ_M,fi` from `EN 1995-1-2` §2.3(2)P.

Incomplete on the substance: never reaches charring (`Abbrandtiefe`, β₀,
d_char) or the reduced cross-section method (§4.2.2), which is the design
method the question asks about. Steps 1–5 all cite the same generic clause,
`EN 1991-1-2` §2.1(1). Nothing R60-specific follows the preamble. None of
the four expected sources was hit.

Not `schlecht`: nothing asserted is false or unsourced.

## Q3 — schlecht

The EN half is right, and better than any previous run: §6.2.3 truss model,
θ strut inclination, V_Rd,max, s_l,max, with `EN 1992-1-1` p. 97 and 98
cited and highlighted.

It fails on the rule that decides this question. The closing sentence claims
*"und den nationalen Festlegungen der ÖNORM B 1992-1-1"* while the only
`B 1992-1-1` source is page 4 — front matter. The national provisions are
asserted, not cited, and a reader has no way to tell.

Defect: the reproduced V_Rd,max formula is garbled (`ν_cw` → `)cw`, `α₁` →
`1` in the underlying extraction), from symbol-font characters the decoder in
`services/symbol_text.py` does not cover.

## Q4 — mittel

Correct EN content: §6.5.4, Bild 6.26/6.27, and k₁–k₄ = 1,0 / 0,85 / 0,75 /
3,0, correctly presented as the **recommended** values with the national
annex to be consulted.

Incomplete: the Austrian values are never given, and the question asked for
them explicitly. Zero `B 1992-1-1` sources beyond front matter.

Not `schlecht`, and this is the distinction the rubric now draws: Q4 does not
pretend to have applied the national annex. It says what it has and what it
lacks. Q3 makes the same omission and covers it with a claim, which is why
the two questions grade differently despite failing on the same gap.

## Q5 — gut

Both recommended values with their parts (`EN 1992-1-1` → 1,0;
`EN 1992-2` → 0,85), the Austrian decision from `B 1992-1-1`, the scope of
each part quoted from context, and a conclusion that follows:
*"muss die Betondruckfestigkeit … in Österreich nicht abgemindert werden."*

Stable — runs 7 and 8 reach the same conclusion, where earlier runs flipped
on context ordering alone.

## What this leaves

Q3 and Q4 fail on the same gap: the `B 1992-1-1` body clauses (§9.2 p. 21,
§9.5 p. 28) are never retrieved. The clause lookup reaches that document only
through its overview table on pages 6–8. For α_cc the overview row happened
to carry the whole answer; for shear and nodes it does not, because those
clauses hold values the table only points at.

The next lever is the same for both: prefer the more specific match in the
clause lookup, so it lands on the clause rather than the summary row. That
refinement was flagged as untested when the lookup was added and still is.

Q2 has been `mittel` in every run to date. Nothing tried so far has addressed
it, and no hypothesis has been formed for why charring content is not
retrieved.
